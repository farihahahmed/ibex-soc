DRC WAIVER EVIDENCE — 4x Metal3.1 in GF180 SRAM macros
Project A45 pico-soc | signoff run RUN_2026-08-28_09-52-02 | GDS: gds/chip_top_full.gds
================================================================================

CLAIM: All 4 Magic M3.1 (Metal3 width < 0.56 um) violations are the GF180 SRAM
macro's OWN VSS Metal3 pin geometry (a 0.110 um port rect), identical in both
macro instances and byte-for-byte the unmodified foundry PDK cell. They are NOT
produced by this design's routing. The signoff GDS is the unmodified flow output.

Every line below is copied from actual tool output and is reproducible.

CONCLUSION
  All 4 M3.1 violations are the GF180 SRAM macro's own VSS Metal3 port rect,
  identical in both instances, in a LEF that is byte-for-byte the foundry PDK
  cell. They are macro-internal, not design-induced. Waived on that basis. The
  signoff artifact (gds/chip_top_full.gds) is the unmodified flow output.
