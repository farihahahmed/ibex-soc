SUMMARY
  The 4 Magic DRC errors are all M3.1 (Metal3 width < minimum) and are the
  GF180 SRAM macro's OWN VSS Metal3 pin geometry — a 0.110 um port rect that
  is below the 0.28 um M3.1 minimum. They are NOT produced by this design's
  routing. Both SRAM instances flag the identical relative rect. Waived as
  foundry macro geometry. The signoff artifact (gds/chip_top_full.gds) is the
  unmodified flow output.

--- 6. Defect is family-wide and upstream is frozen --------------------------
  The 0.110 um VSS Metal3 port rect is present in every macro in the family
  (sram64x8, sram128x8, sram256x8, sram512x8). This is a known GF180 foundry-IP
  issue: the upstream repo
    google/globalfoundries-pdk-ip-gf180mcu_fd_ip_sram
  has a standing OPEN DRC issue (#9, filed by a GF180 maintainer, 2022, never
  fixed) and was ARCHIVED read-only on 2026-04-18. No fixed macro exists to
  switch to; waiving is the correct and only path. Our macro is md5-identical
  to that final upstream release.
